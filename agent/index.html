Frequently asked questions

What is a preparation agent?
准备 agent 是什么？
It is an interactive workspace that turns a few facts about your organisation — standard, industry, lab type, stage and scope — into a personalised preparation dossier. Instead of a generic checklist, you get the gaps that actually matter for your situation, ranked by severity, with the tools and documents that close each one.
Is the agent free?
agent 免费吗？
Yes. The agent and your readiness dossier are free, with no login required. You only pay when you choose to generate a specific document, run an uncertainty report, or engage a preparation package — and the reality diagnosis is permanently free.
Does the agent replace certification or accreditation?
agent 能替代认证／认可吗？
No. It organises your preparation and links you to the tools — it does not issue certificates, accreditation decisions or audit conclusions. Always verify outputs against the current standard text and your accreditation body’s rules.
Which standards are covered?
覆盖哪些标准？
ISO 9001 (2015 and 2026), ISO 14001 (2015 and 2026), ISO/IEC 17025 (2017), ISO 45001 (2018) and IATF 16949 (2016). Pick your standard and edition to launch the matching agent.
